Make the plan workable

The operating model.

Weekly rhythm

  • 2–3 hours: concepts and current SAP learning material.
  • 4–6 hours: build, deploy, test, and document.
  • 1 hour: explain the work aloud and record gaps.
  • Move on only after the stage gate is demonstrable.

Practice environments

  • Local: Node, CAP, Python, Docker, mocked services. Use from Stages 1–3.
  • BTP Trial: Cloud Foundry, BAS, CAP, APIs, destinations, security.
  • Generative AI Hub Basic Trial: AI Launchpad, orchestration, grounding.
  • Learning Hub or corporate landscape: serious practice and certification.

Provision before the advanced stages

For Stages 4–9, plan for a controlled BTP training subaccount with AI Core Extended, AI Launchpad, Generative AI Hub, HANA Cloud, Build / Build Code, Joule entitlement, Joule Studio, and a controlled SAP backend. AI Core Free or Standard alone does not cover Generative AI Hub scenarios; Joule Studio needs the appropriate SAP Build and Joule licensing.

How to assess real skill

LevelEvidence
FoundationExplains the concepts and completes guided exercises.
PractitionerModifies and builds a solution with limited guidance.
EngineerIndependently designs, builds, secures, deploys, evaluates, and troubleshoots.
ConsultantIdentifies the right problem, chooses the right pattern, explains business value and risk, and defends the architecture.

Credentials

  • Required: SAP Certified Associate – SAP Generative AI Developer (C_AIG).
  • Strongly recommended: SAP Certified – Backend Developer – SAP CAP.
  • Optional later: relevant Joule / Business AI achievements and architecture / integration certification.

Final proof

The capstone is the real qualification: a working enterprise AI solution plus a consultant-style defense of why the architecture exists, what it controls, what it improves, and where it should not be used.
